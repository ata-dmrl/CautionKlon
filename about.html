Our story

Why we built Caution

Caution started with a problem we kept running into: critical software still ran on blind trust.

It grew out of our work at Distrust, the security consulting and open source R&D firm founded by Lance and Anton, where we helped hedge funds, custodians, blockchain teams, cloud platforms and grid operators secure systems responsible for more than $600B in assets.

Again and again we saw mission-critical infrastructure operated as a black box, with little ability to independently check what was actually executing in production. Teams needed reproducible builds, verifiable deployment and cryptographic proof of running code.

So we built Caution.
